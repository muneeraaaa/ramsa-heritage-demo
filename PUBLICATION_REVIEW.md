- [ ] Check AI-generated concept visuals for fabricated government logos, fake factual figures and inaccurate cultural details; label as concept art.
- [ ] Do not upload participant birthdates or contact information from slides.
- [ ] Confirm accurate role and ownership of any official organization named; no partnership may be implied.
- [ ] Keep "student prototype / illustrative data" notice visible on all role pages.
- [ ] Do not call this a real UAE government dashboard or a verified AI risk score.
- [ ] Verify pearl-diving and palm craft historical terms with cultural specialists.
- [ ] Check Arabic/English navigation, media captions and accessible alternatives.
- [ ] Check final Pages URL in Chrome and mobile.
- [ ] Never add API keys or passwords to the repository.
